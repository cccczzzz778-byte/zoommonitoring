Support

Buxoro Meeting Monitor is an administrative Zoom meeting monitoring application.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that the Zoom account is authorized in the application.
  • Confirm that the meeting ID and password are correct.
  • For external-account meetings, confirm that the authorized Zoom user is already present in the meeting and that the application is approved for the required Zoom Meeting SDK use case.
  • Refresh the monitoring page and try again.

Support contact

Email: aasorov07@gmail.com

Please include the time of the issue, the error message shown, and whether the meeting was hosted by the same or an external Zoom account. Do not send Zoom passwords, client secrets, access tokens, or other credentials by email.

Security reports

If you believe you found a security issue, send a concise description to the support email above and avoid including sensitive user data unless necessary.
